Utah

Utah is the 45th state in the United States. It was admitted on January 4, 1896. Utah has 5 national parks, which people call the Mighty 5. There is Arches, Bryce Canyon, Canyonlands, Capitol Reef, and Zion. At Disneyland's Soarin' Around the World ride, Utah is featured. It shows part of Monument Valley, which is located on the southerneastern corner of Utah, on the border with Arizona.
